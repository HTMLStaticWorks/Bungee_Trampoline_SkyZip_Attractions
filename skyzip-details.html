Attraction Highlights

SkyZip Line

Prepare to fly across the park with our state-of-the-art SkyZip Line system! This attraction is designed to give you a true bird's-eye view, launching from a 40-foot custom steel tower and zipping across a 500-foot-long high-tensile cable at speeds reaching up to 35 MPH.

Engineered with the industry-leading zipStop eddy current magnetic braking system, riders experience a smooth, automated decelerating slowdown with no manual braking needed. It is a premium, safe, and breathtaking experience suitable for adults and children alike.

500-foot gliding run
Magnetic auto-brake technology
Panoramas of the entire park
Dual safety cable anchors

Technical Specifications

Cable Length 500 Feet (approx. 152 Meters)
Launch Height 40 Feet (approx. 12 Meters) high tower
Top Velocity Up to 35 MPH (56 Km/h)
Brake Mechanism zipStop magnetic eddy-current auto-retracting brakes
Weight Range Min: 60 lbs / Max: 250 lbs (27 kg to 113 kg)

The Flight Process

1

Gear & Harness Check

Our certified instructors fit you with an impact-rated helmet, heavy-duty gloves, and a full-body overhead safety harness.

2

Tower Launch

Ascend the launch platform tower. An instructor clips your trolley to the primary steel line, locks the safety backup, and clears you for flight.

3

Automatic Deceleration

Glide at exhilarating speeds across the tree line. As you approach the landing dock, the magnetic brakes engage automatically for a smooth, gentle stop.
