Attraction Highlights

Bungee Trampoline

Experience the ultimate sensation of high-flying flips with our premium Bungee Trampoline system! Perfectly engineered for both safety and high adrenaline, this attraction combines the power of high-performance trampolines with custom-tuned elastic bungee cords to launch you up to 25 feet in the air.

Whether you are a beginner looking to experience weightlessness or an advanced thrill-seeker practicing front and back flips, our certified instructors adjust the tension to match your weight and comfort level, helping you pull off amazing acrobatics with total ease and peace of mind.

Zero experience needed
All ages (5+ and 40 lbs+)
Professional safety harness
1-on-1 instructor coaching

Technical Specifications

Max Jump Height 25 Feet (approx. 7.6 Meters)
Elastic Cord Setup Multi-strand latex rubber with double safety webbing cores
Harness System Dual-redundant padded waist and leg loops (Petzl/Edelrid)
Trampoline Surface Heavy-duty commercial-grade jump bed with safety padding
Weight Range Min: 40 lbs / Max: 220 lbs (18 kg to 100 kg)

The Jump Process

1

Secure Harnessing

Our team fits you into a heavy-duty padded harness designed to distribute weight comfortably across your waist and hips.

2

Cord Tensioning

Certified bungee cords are attached to your sides and raised via automatic electric winches to match your specific height and weight.

3

Taking Flight

Start jumping! The elastic assists your bounce, allowing you to soar higher with every jump and rotate into flips safely.
