Why Riders Choose Us

We don't just operate rides — we engineer unforgettable moments built on trust, technology, and world-class safety standards.

Industry-Leading Safety

All our equipment meets or exceeds ASTM international amusement ride standards. Every harness, bungee cord, and zip trolley is inspected by certified technicians before every single operating day. Safety is non-negotiable here.

Expert Certified Staff

Our instructors are trained and certified in both ride operations and first aid. They guide every rider through a personalised safety briefing and remain attentive throughout the session, ready to assist and encourage participants of all skill levels.

All Ages Welcome

From excited 5-year-olds on the Bungee Trampoline to competitive adults on the SkyZip Line, our attractions are designed with flexibility in mind. Family groups, school trips, birthday parties, and corporate teams are all catered for.

Easy Online Booking

Skip the queue entirely. Our streamlined online booking system lets you choose your experience, select a time slot, complete your digital waiver, and pay — all before you arrive. Instant confirmation, zero hassle.

Advanced Equipment

We invest in the very best ride technology. The SkyZip features the renowned zipStop® eddy-current magnetic braking system — the same technology used in top-tier adventure parks worldwide. Smooth, reliable, and completely hands-free.

Memory-Making Moments

Preparation Guide

What to Expect On Your Visit

Follow our quick preparation checklist to ensure a seamless, stress-free experience from the moment you arrive to the moment you take off into the sky.

1

Book Online First

Reserve your timed slot online to skip the queue entirely. Pre-booking guarantees your session and lets you complete your digital safety waiver before arriving — so you spend less time waiting and more time flying.

2

Wear Right Clothes

Dress for action — comfortable, form-fitting activewear and close-toed athletic shoes are mandatory for all riders. Avoid skirts, dresses, open-toed shoes, or loose jewelry that could get caught in the harness.

3

Check-in On Time

Arrive at least 15 minutes before your session begins. Our friendly reception team will verify your waiver, scan your booking confirmation, and direct you to your attraction zone — quick and seamless every time.

4

Harness & Fly

Your instructor will fit your full-body harness, perform a mandatory double-check, walk you through your personalised safety briefing, and then launch you into the best moments of your day. The thrill begins!

First Time Here?

Don't worry — every visit starts with a personalized safety orientation from one of our certified instructors. We walk first-timers through everything from harness fitting to basic technique, so you feel completely confident before you leave the ground. No prior experience necessary.

Your Safety First

Mandatory Safety Instructions

The safety of every rider is our absolute top priority. Please read and follow these instructions before participating in any attraction. Non-compliance may result in removal from the ride without refund.

Proper Attire

Wear closed-toe athletic shoes and comfortable, form-fitting activewear. Skirts, dresses, open-toed footwear, and loose-fitting clothing are strictly prohibited for rider safety.

Health Notice

Our rides are not suitable for pregnant women, or individuals with recent back, neck, or joint injuries, heart conditions, or chronic medical conditions. If in doubt, please consult your doctor before participating.

Sign Waiver

A signed digital waiver is mandatory for all participants without exception. Minors (under 18) must have their waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian. Waivers can be completed in advance online — we recommend doing this before arriving.
