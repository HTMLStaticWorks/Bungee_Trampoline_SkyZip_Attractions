Why Our Harness Systems Are The Safest in the Industry

May 10, 2026 David Chen

When you're suspended 25 feet in the air, or zipping across a park at 30mph, trust is everything. At SkyZip, we don't just meet industry safety standards—we completely redefine them.

The Dual-Redundancy Principle

Every single attachment point in our Bungee Trampoline and SkyZip systems is double-secured. If one carabiner or strap were to theoretically fail, a secondary system is already actively engaged. We use military-grade nylon webbing that is tested to hold up to 5,000 lbs of force.

"Safety isn't a feature of our park; it's the entire foundation our park is built upon." - David Chen, Lead Safety Officer

Daily Inspection Protocols

Before the first guest even steps foot in the park, our engineering team conducts a 45-point inspection of every single trampoline cord, zip line cable, and harness. Any piece of equipment showing even a fraction of a millimeter of wear is immediately retired and replaced.

We believe that true thrills come from knowing you are 100% secure. Ready to experience it for yourself? Book your slot today.

David Chen

Lead Safety Officer

David has over 15 years of experience in structural engineering and high-altitude safety rigging.
